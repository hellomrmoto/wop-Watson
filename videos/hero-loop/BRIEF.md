## Intent

Hero background loop for the WOP Watson artist site (hip-hop / rapper). It sits behind a giant
name set in the page's HTML, so the clip itself carries **no text**. Muted and moody, never
neon: warm black, tobacco, oxblood, brass, bone. Loops seamlessly (frame 0 continues from the
last frame). Plays muted, full-bleed, under a dark gradient and the site's own film grain.

## Customizations

- Ambient layers: smoke/haze, a slow stage-light beam, drifting dust, film grain (kept light, the
  site adds its own), one jewelry-glint flare.
- Keep the focal light upper-right; keep the lower-left dark and quiet (the page title sits there).

## Notes

- `flow: automation` / `storyboard: no` were locked from a "just make the clip" request, not asked.
- No photographic media: nothing for `/media-use` treatments to act on.
- Dark gradients band under H.264. Radial falloffs and a dither-level grain only; no full-screen
  linear gradients.
- Target file size for the site hero slot: 6 MB or less, H.264 MP4, no audio track.
